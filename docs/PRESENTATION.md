# Client Presentation

Hi [Client name],

## Demo caveats to state clearly

- Brand name, film titles, locations, services, and copy are illustrative.
- Films are short generated sample edits from licensed photography, not client work.
- The inquiry form does not deliver email or store data.
- No testimonials, awards, client names, or business credentials are claimed.
