
# THEME.MD — Bali Wedding Films

## 1. Creative Direction

Create a premium, cinematic, editorial-style website for a Bali wedding videography and video-editing business.

The visual identity should communicate:
- Romance and emotion
- Luxury destination weddings
- Cinematic storytelling
- Professional video editing
- Timeless elegance
- Trust and creative craftsmanship

The website must feel like a luxury wedding film studio, not a generic business landing page or SaaS template.

## 2. Design Philosophy

Use a cinematic editorial layout inspired by luxury wedding magazines, destination wedding photography, and independent filmmaking studios.

Design principles:
- Large cinematic imagery and video.
- Generous whitespace.
- Elegant serif typography.
- Minimalist navigation.
- Warm neutral colors.
- Asymmetric editorial compositions.
- Smooth, restrained animations.
- Strong visual hierarchy.
- Clear and tasteful calls to action.

Avoid:
- Generic gradients.
- Excessive rounded cards.
- Bright neon colors.
- Overly complex layouts.
- Excessive animations.
- Unnecessary glassmorphism.
- Generic stock-template aesthetics.
- Dense blocks of text.

## 3. Color Palette

### Primary Colors

- Ivory: #F8F5EF
- Warm White: #FFFDF9
- Sand Beige: #D9C7AE
- Muted Olive: #69705A
- Deep Charcoal: #282923
- Soft Black: #171815
- Warm Gray: #8C887F
- Light Border: #E6E0D6

### Usage

- Main background: Ivory.
- Alternate section background: Warm White.
- Main text: Deep Charcoal.
- Secondary text: Warm Gray.
- Primary buttons: Deep Charcoal with Ivory text.
- Secondary accents: Muted Olive.
- Dividers and borders: Light Border.
- Dark cinematic sections: Soft Black.

Maintain consistent contrast and readability.

## 4. Typography

### Display Typography

Preferred font: Cormorant Garamond.

Use for:
- Hero headlines.
- Section titles.
- Featured film titles.
- Editorial statements.
- Large pull quotes.

Typography characteristics:
- Elegant.
- Refined.
- Romantic.
- High contrast.
- Editorial.

### Body Typography

Preferred font: Inter.

Use for:
- Paragraphs.
- Navigation.
- Buttons.
- Forms.
- Metadata.
- Service descriptions.

Typography characteristics:
- Clean.
- Readable.
- Minimal.
- Modern.

### Typography Rules

- Use large headlines with deliberate line breaks.
- Keep paragraph widths comfortable.
- Use letter spacing sparingly.
- Use uppercase labels for small editorial captions.
- Establish consistent heading sizes across breakpoints.
- Avoid excessive font weights.

## 5. Layout System

### Container

- Use a consistent centered content container.
- Suggested maximum width: 1440px.
- Main content width: approximately 1200px.
- Use generous horizontal padding.
- Maintain consistent vertical rhythm.

### Spacing

Use a consistent spacing scale based on 4px increments.

Suggested values:
- 4px: micro spacing.
- 8px: compact spacing.
- 16px: standard spacing.
- 24px: component spacing.
- 32px: section content spacing.
- 64px: large spacing.
- 96px: major section spacing.
- 128px: cinematic editorial spacing on large screens.

Adjust spacing for mobile devices.

### Borders and Corners

- Prefer subtle 1px borders.
- Use square or slightly rounded corners.
- Avoid excessive pill-shaped containers.
- Buttons may use subtle corner rounding.
- Video thumbnails should prioritize the composition of the image.

## 6. Navigation

Create a minimalist navigation bar containing:
- Brand wordmark.
- Films.
- Services.
- About.
- Contact.
- Inquire CTA.

Behavior:
- Transparent over the hero when appropriate.
- Switch to a readable background after scrolling if implemented.
- Smooth anchor navigation.
- Functional mobile menu.
- Visible keyboard focus.
- Clear hover states.

Keep navigation visually lightweight.

## 7. Hero Section

The hero is the most important visual component.

Requirements:
- Full-width cinematic video or high-quality poster image.
- Destination wedding imagery featuring Bali-inspired tropical scenery.
- Dark overlay where needed for text contrast.
- Large editorial headline.
- Short supporting copy.
- Primary CTA: Watch Our Films.
- Secondary CTA: Inquire Now.
- Optional subtle scroll indicator.

Suggested headline:

"Your Story, Beautifully Told."

Suggested supporting copy:

"Cinematic wedding films shaped by emotion, light, and the moments that matter most."

Use this copy as demo content until the actual client's brand messaging is available.

The hero must look impressive on desktop and remain fast and readable on mobile.

## 8. Featured Films

Create an editorial portfolio rather than a generic grid of cards.

Requirements:
- Three to six featured sample films.
- Large cinematic thumbnails.
- Custom play buttons.
- Elegant title and location metadata.
- Consistent image treatment.
- Responsive grid or editorial layout.
- Accessible video modal or inline playback.

Example demo titles:
- A Wedding Above the Ocean.
- Golden Hour in Uluwatu.
- An Intimate Garden Celebration.
- A Day to Remember.

These are illustrative titles, not verified client projects.

Do not fabricate client names, testimonials, awards, or real wedding credentials.

## 9. Services Section

Present services using clean typography and restrained visual elements.

Possible services:
- Wedding Highlight Films.
- Full-Length Wedding Films.
- Short-Form Wedding Reels.
- Color Grading.
- Wedding Video Post-Production.
- Custom Editing Packages.

Only present services that the client confirms they offer as actual services. Otherwise, label them as illustrative demo content.

Keep descriptions concise and benefit-oriented.

## 10. About Section

Create an emotionally engaging editorial section introducing the creative philosophy.

Visual direction:
- Large supporting image.
- Asymmetric text and image composition.
- Restrained typography.
- Generous negative space.

Focus on storytelling, atmosphere, detail, and preserving meaningful moments.

Do not invent the company's history, location, team members, credentials, or experience.

## 11. Workflow Section

Use a simple four-step process:

1. Connect — Discuss the project and requirements.
2. Prepare — Organize footage and creative direction.
3. Create — Edit, color-grade, and refine the film.
4. Deliver — Provide the agreed final output.

Adapt this process to the actual service model.

## 12. Contact Section

Create a visually distinctive inquiry section.

Requirements:
- Name.
- Email address.
- Service interest.
- Event date, optional.
- Project details.
- Submit button.
- Clear validation messages.
- Success and error states.

Use a refined layout with clear labels and accessible form controls.

The demo must not claim that an inquiry was sent unless a working backend or email integration confirms submission.

## 13. Motion and Interaction

Use subtle, purposeful motion.

Preferred effects:
- Gentle fade-in.
- Small vertical reveal.
- Smooth scrolling.
- Image hover zoom.
- Underline transitions.
- Subtle button transitions.
- Video modal fade.
- Restrained section reveals.

Rules:
- Use Framer Motion only where it adds value.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
- Avoid excessive parallax.
- Avoid distracting continuous animations.
- Ensure all controls remain responsive.

## 14. Responsive Design

### Desktop
- Large cinematic hero.
- Wide editorial compositions.
- Generous whitespace.
- Large portfolio thumbnails.

### Tablet
- Reduced section spacing.
- Balanced text and image layouts.
- Responsive portfolio columns.

### Mobile
- Single-column editorial layouts.
- Readable typography.
- Compact navigation.
- Touch-friendly buttons.
- Optimized video posters.
- No horizontal overflow.
- Avoid loading heavy autoplay video.
- Keep inquiry CTA easy to find.

## 15. Media Guidelines

- Use high-quality, legally usable images and video.
- Prefer optimized WebP or AVIF images.
- Use poster images for video backgrounds.
- Lazy-load below-the-fold media.
- Avoid downloading large videos unnecessarily.
- Provide fallbacks for failed media.
- Never rely on images alone to communicate important information.
- Do not use assets that misrepresent the client as their creator.

## 16. Accessibility

- Use semantic HTML.
- Maintain sufficient color contrast.
- Add meaningful image alt text.
- Ensure keyboard navigation.
- Provide visible focus states.
- Label all form fields.
- Support reduced motion.
- Ensure accessible modal behavior.
- Use descriptive link and button labels.

## 17. Brand Consistency

All sections must share:
- The same typography system.
- The same color palette.
- Consistent container widths.
- Consistent spacing.
- Consistent button styles.
- Consistent animation timing.
- Consistent editorial tone.

The final result should feel like one cohesive luxury wedding film brand.

## 18. Final Visual Standard

The website should feel:
- Cinematic.
- Premium.
- Romantic.
- Timeless.
- Editorial.
- Minimal.
- Professional.
- Emotionally engaging.

Prioritize a polished first impression and excellent media presentation over unnecessary features.
