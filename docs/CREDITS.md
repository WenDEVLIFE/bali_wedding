## Video

`public/films/hero-loop.mp4` and `public/films/film-1.mp4` through `film-4.mp4`
are locally generated, silent, ten-second (hero: eight-second) Ken Burns-style
edits made from the photographs listed above. They are **demo films**, not client
work, and should be replaced with the studio's own footage before launch.
